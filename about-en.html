What is BunDeal

Save more together.

BunDeal shows reviewed offers and, after login, sends you to the merchant through an affiliate link. The card says what the offer includes: cashback, a referral, or a creator campaign.

How an offer works

1

Open the offer

You see the merchant price and the offer type.

2

Sign in and buy

Signing in before the merchant click connects the click with the later transaction.

3

Share only when it belongs

A personal link exists for Referral and Creator, and only after your own purchase.

4

Wait for confirmation

Cashback comes only from confirmed commission. Returns create no payment.
