Accessing the website

When the website is accessed, the hosting provider processes technically necessary connection data, particularly the IP address, date, time, requested page and browser type. Email

If you email info@bundeal.de, we use the information you provide to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the message concerns a specific BunDeal enquiry, and otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Messages remain stored for as long as required to process the enquiry.

Cookies and audience measurement

This prototype does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising tools. Fonts are served directly by this website and are not loaded from an external provider.
