Login connects purchase and referral

You are logged in to BunDeal before clicking through to the merchant. BunDeal stores the deal, round, click and direct referrer. The network reports the transaction status later.

Calculation after confirmation

Assumption: each retained purchase generates €10 confirmed commission. Payment is released only after the affiliate network confirms the relevant purchase.

Your own confirmed purchase

35% of €10 produces €3.50 base cashback.

Three direct referrals

3 × 15% of €10 produces an additional €4.50 referral bonus.

Your payment in this example

€3.50 base cashback plus a €4.50 referral bonus produces a total of €8.00.

No referral chain

If a referred buyer later refers someone else, only that buyer receives the direct bonus. You receive nothing from the second level.

Conditions in this example

  • The deal remains online while the offer, price and affiliate link are current. Each purchase belongs to a clearly defined monthly round.
  • Attribution is possible only when the buyer is logged in to BunDeal before clicking through and affiliate tracking works correctly.
  • After an attributed purchase, the user may receive a personal referral link. Only direct referrals are rewarded.
  • A full return or rejection reduces the confirmed commission for that purchase to zero. It therefore generates neither base cashback nor a referral bonus.
  • The bonus is a possible payment from BunDeal, not a merchant discount or price reduction.
  • The example allocation of 35% / 15% / 50% must be reviewed contractually, commercially and for tax purposes before a pilot.
