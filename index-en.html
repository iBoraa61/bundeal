Purchase from the merchant

BunDeal does not operate a shop, shopping basket or delivery service. Ordering, payment, shipping and returns remain with the merchant.

Refer personally and directly

After an attributed purchase, you receive a personal link in your BunDeal account. Only directly referred confirmed purchases can increase your referral bonus.

Only confirmed commission counts

Pending purchases are not cashback. Base cashback and referral bonuses are released only after confirmation by the affiliate network.

How a deal works

Deals remain online while the offer and affiliate link are current. Each purchase is assigned to a monthly round for clear settlement.

1

Open the deal

You see the merchant price, current monthly round and example allocation of confirmed commission.

2

Log in and purchase

Logging in before the merchant click allows the BunDeal click to be matched with the reported transaction.

3

Share your direct link

After an attributed purchase, you can share your personal deal link. There are no multi-level referrals.

4

Await confirmation

Cashback is calculated only from confirmed commission. Returns and rejected transactions generate no payment.

A simple model without tiers

The bonus is not a merchant discount. It is a possible later payment from BunDeal, funded by confirmed affiliate commission.

Base cashback for the buyer

In the example, 35% of confirmed commission is paid to the buyer as base cashback. The exact amount depends on the commission actually confirmed.

Bonus for direct referrals

In the example, a further 15% is paid to the direct referrer. Without direct attribution there is no referral bonus. Referrals of referrals are not rewarded.

Starting with a focused community

BunDeal initially creates the deals and reviews the merchant, price, affiliate rules and link. Users may submit suitable offers, but publication follows only after review.

Personal statistics

Your future account will show pending and confirmed purchases, base cashback and your directly referred confirmed purchases.

Optional badges

Badges may highlight useful deal suggestions and helpful sharing. Public financial data or real names are not required.

Rankings may follow later

If rankings are introduced, they will be optional and preferably anonymised. Correct attribution and settlement come first in the MVP.
